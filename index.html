How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server — your picture stays on your own phone or computer from start to finish.

1

Give a picture or a description

Upload any photo — your product, your dish, your shop. No photo? Describe the product in words and pick the ready-made visual that fits it best.

2

Add your words

Your business name, one strong headline, your best points and the final call-to-action. Pick 15 to 60 seconds, widescreen or vertical, and a text style — the preview updates live as you type.

3

Generate & download

The studio plays your ad once while recording it with music, then gives you the finished video file to download and post wherever you sell.
