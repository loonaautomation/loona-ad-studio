About Loona Ad Studio

Loona Ad Studio · Loona Automation

Loona Ad Studio turns a product photo — or even just a description — into a finished video ad. Upload a picture of what you sell, add a headline, your best points and an ending line, choose widescreen or vertical, and the studio builds a complete ad with animated text, background music and a call-to-action ending: 15 to 60 seconds, ready to download as a video file and post anywhere. No sign-up, and your photo never leaves your device.

What it does

Every ad is composed from the ingredients a real ad needs. Your photo (or a ready-made visual for food, fashion, tech, delivery or services, if you have no photo yet) carries the picture; your business name and headline carry the message; up to three selling points appear one by one so the viewer actually reads them; and the ending screen carries your call to action — a phone number, an address, an offer — the part most homemade ads forget. Three text styles (Bold, Classic, Playful) change the personality of the ad, the music sets its mood, and the format switch chooses between widescreen 16:9 for YouTube and Facebook and vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. A live preview shows the ad exactly as it will look while you type, so there are no surprises after generating.

How it works — honestly

The whole studio runs in your browser. Your photo is loaded on your own device and never uploaded to a server — there is no server in this process at all. When you press Generate, the studio plays the ad through second by second and records it into a video file, which is why generating takes roughly as long as the ad itself. The finished file downloads to your device, and it is yours: post it on your pages, send it on WhatsApp, run it as an ad — no watermark, no permission to ask.

Who it is for

Small businesses that need a decent ad today: the food place with a new dish, the boutique with new stock, the repair shop, the tutor, the delivery service. Owners who currently post a plain photo with a caption — or nothing at all — because a video editor costs money they would rather spend on stock. Ad Studio closes that gap in about a minute, free.

What it cannot do

It is a template studio, not a film crew. It will not shoot your product, write a clever script for you, or match a bespoke agency edit with custom animation — the layouts are fixed so the result is fast and consistent. And it cannot rescue a bad photograph: a dark, blurry photo makes a dark, blurry ad, which is why the studio asks for a clear, bright picture. Within those honest limits, what it produces is a clean, complete, correctly-sized video ad that most small businesses would otherwise simply never have.

Why this is free

Loona Automation's founding belief is that the door in front of a tool matters as much as the tool: accounts, paywalls and trials that expire mid-job are where most people give up. Loona Ad Studio is free because it runs on your device — your photo, your ad, your file, with no rendering server to pay for. The site is supported by Loona Automation and may show advertising, but using it costs nothing.

Who builds it

Loona Ad Studio is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona — a school teacher and AI teacher trainer in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan — as one part of a network of 918 free tools published in 40 languages. The network's rule is simple: tools should be free, fast and open to everyone, with no sign-up wall between a person and the job they came to do.
