Loona Ad Studio — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona Ad Studio · Loona Automation

Is Loona Ad Studio free?

Yes — completely. Build the ad, preview it, generate it and download the video file, with no sign-up and no watermark. The studio runs in your browser and is supported by Loona Automation.

Does my photo get uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser on your own device — your photo is never uploaded, stored or seen by anyone. There is no server in the process; when you press Generate, your own device records the ad into a video file.

How long can my ad be?

15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. Shorter ads suit a single offer or announcement; longer ones give your three selling points room to land one by one. Generating takes roughly as long as the ad itself, because the studio records it second by second.

Which format should I choose — 16:9 or 9:16?

Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, where the phone screen is tall. Widescreen 16:9 for YouTube, Facebook pages and anything shown on a TV or computer screen. If you post in both places, generate the ad twice, once in each format — it takes a minute.

I don't have a product photo. Can I still make an ad?

Yes. Describe your product or business in words, or start from a ready-made visual — food and restaurant, fashion, tech, delivery, business — and the studio composes the ad around it. A real photo of your own product will always beat a stock visual when you have one.

What makes an ad from this studio look good?

A clear, bright photo; a short headline a person can read in two seconds; no more than three selling points, each a few words; and an ending line that tells the viewer exactly what to do — a phone number, an address or a dated offer. The preview shows the real result as you type, so judge it there.

Why does generating take as long as the ad?

Because the studio records your ad by playing it — second by second — inside your browser and capturing it as a video file. Keep the tab open and in front while it records; switching away can interrupt the capture on some browsers.

Can I use the finished ad for my business?

Yes. The video is yours — post it on your pages, send it to customers, or run it as a paid ad. It is made from your photo and your words, on your device, and it carries no watermark and no conditions from us.
